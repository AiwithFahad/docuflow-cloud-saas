FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my company data separated from other companies?

Yes. DocuFlow is multi-tenant: every record and file carries your company ID, and authorization is checked on every request and every file operation.

Where are files stored?

In private storage outside the public web root, with randomized names — served only through authenticated, permission-checked downloads or expiring share tokens.

Can I try before paying?

Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial. Paid plans are invoiced and recorded as pending until payment is verified.

What file types are supported?

PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images, text, CSV and ZIP — validated by extension and real content type, with per-plan size limits.
